Ashwini Clothing V23 — Product UX + Gallery Fixes

1. Size buttons now toggle: click selected size again to unselect it.
2. Add to Cart now gives visible feedback: button changes to “✓ Added to Cart” and a toast shows the selected size.
3. Product-detail Add to Cart gives the same visible feedback before closing the modal.
4. Slider “Shop Now” buttons are now functional and take the customer to the related category.
5. Product detail now supports a maximum of 5 gallery photos with thumbnails and main-photo switching.
6. Gallery data is stored in the products table for future multi-photo product uploads.
7. Existing bundled product assets are preserved; no fake “different-side” photos were invented where genuine alternate photos were not available.
8. Existing cart, checkout, slider, category and modal functions are preserved.
