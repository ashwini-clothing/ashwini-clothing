Ashwini Clothing V37 — Seasonal Offer + Customer Offer Notifications

Added without changing existing core flows:
- Admin Offer Management: create, edit, activate/deactivate, delete.
- Seasonal offer popup with banner, title, description, coupon, dates and Shop Now action.
- Customer Offers panel for logged-in and logged-out visitors.
- In-app customer offer notifications for registered customer accounts.
- Admin Send Offer action creates a notification for all registered customers.
- Customer notifications can be marked as read.

Note: Actual SMS/email delivery requires a configured SMS/email provider and credentials. This version safely stores and displays offer notifications inside the website; it does not pretend to send external SMS/email without a provider.
