Shipping Policy

Effective and last updated: 29 August 2026

Orders are dispatched from the Ashwini Clothing service area in Jandli / Ambala Cantt, Haryana. Delivery availability and estimates are checked using the delivery PIN code and current store settings.

Estimated delivery time

  • Jandli / Ambala Cantt area: generally 1–2 business days.
  • Other Haryana locations: generally 2–4 business days.
  • Nearby states: generally 3–5 business days.
  • Other serviceable Indian locations: generally 5–8 business days.
  • Remote locations: generally 7–10 business days.

These are estimates shown by the current delivery configuration, not guaranteed arrival dates. The estimate displayed for your PIN code at checkout should be treated as the current estimate.

Address and serviceability

Provide a complete recipient name, 10-digit mobile number, address, city, state and 6-digit PIN code. You are responsible for checking these details before ordering. Some PIN codes, cities or states may be unavailable or temporarily blocked.

Charges and dispatch

Any shipping charge will be shown before order confirmation. Dispatch begins only after the order is accepted and, for online orders, payment is securely confirmed. Orders placed on holidays or outside processing hours may begin processing on the next business day.

Delay, failed delivery or damage

Courier delays can occur because of weather, public events, transport disruption, address problems or remote-location conditions. If tracking is stalled, delivery fails, or a parcel arrives visibly damaged, contact ashwiniweb88@gmail.com promptly with the order number and supporting photographs where relevant.

Risk and inspection

Inspect the parcel soon after delivery. Do not accept a clearly opened or severely damaged package without noting the condition with the delivery partner where possible. Product return/replacement eligibility is governed by the Refund Policy and the in-account return window.
